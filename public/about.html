ABOUT STORY TELLER

More Than Just Stories

Story Teller is an interactive storytelling experience created for children, teenagers, and adults.

From Fantasy and Comedy to Horror, Thriller, Romance, Moral stories, and many more worlds, Story Teller gives readers a simple and enjoyable way to discover something new.

Reading becomes more personal with features such as Read Aloud, bookmarks, favourite stories, reading history, customizable fonts, age-based collections, and light and dark reading modes.

📖

A Story Is a Door

Open it, and you never know what world you might discover on the other side.
